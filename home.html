RIZAL’S LIFE: HIGHER EDUCATION AND LIFE ABROAD

ACTIVITY #4

TRAVEL MAP

Travel Map

We will see how did he go from here, there, and everywhere

NO. YEAR / DATE COUNTRY / LOCATION DESCRIPTION / SIGNIFICANCE
1 1877–1882 Manila, Philippines University of Santo Tomas (UST) — Rizal studied Philosophy and Letters and later Medicine. He eventually decided to continue his medical education in Spain.
2 May 3, 1882 Manila → Singapore Rizal secretly left the Philippines to continue his studies in Spain, particularly to study medicine at the Universidad Central de Madrid. He traveled under the name José Mercado.
3 May 9, 1882 Singapore Transit stop. Rizal's ship arrived in Singapore. He went around the city and observed its surroundings before continuing his journey to Europe.
4 May 17–18, 1882 Point Galle & Colombo, Ceylon (Sri Lanka) Transit and observation. Rizal recorded observations of the places he encountered during his voyage to Europe.
5 May 27, 1882 Aden Transit and observation. Rizal stopped at Aden during his voyage toward the Suez Canal and Europe.
6 June 2–7, 1882 Suez / Port Said, Egypt Transit to Europe. Rizal passed through the Suez Canal while traveling toward the Mediterranean and Europe.
7 June 11, 1882 Naples, Italy First European ground visited. Rizal briefly went around Naples and observed the city before continuing to France.
8 June 12–15, 1882 Marseilles, France Transit and sightseeing. Rizal stayed briefly in Marseilles and visited places such as the Château d'If before traveling by train to Barcelona.
9 June 16, 1882 Barcelona, Spain Beginning of his life in Europe. Rizal stayed in Barcelona and wrote “El Amor Patrio”, an early article expressing his love for his homeland.
10 Sept. 2, 1882 Madrid, Spain Higher education. Rizal enrolled at the Universidad Central de Madrid to continue studying Medicine and also pursued Philosophy and Letters.
11 1882–1885 Madrid, Spain Medical and liberal-arts education. He continued his university studies while becoming involved with Filipino reformists and the Propaganda Movement.
12 June 1883 Paris, France Medical observation/training. Rizal spent about three months in France observing medical practices and visiting hospitals. He wanted to broaden his knowledge of medicine.
13 1884 Madrid, Spain Medical education completed at the undergraduate/licentiate level. Rizal earned his Licentiate in Medicine in Madrid. He continued developing his knowledge and interests in medicine, literature, and reform.
14 1885 Paris, France Ophthalmology training. After his medical studies, Rizal went to Paris to gain further experience in ophthalmology, the medical specialty involving the eyes.
15 Feb. 1886 Heidelberg, Germany Ophthalmology specialization/training. Rizal continued his medical training, particularly in ophthalmology, and studied under Dr. Otto Becker. He also became interested in German science and culture.
16 1886 Wilhelmsfeld, Germany Study and writing. Rizal stayed in Wilhelmsfeld and continued writing and studying German culture and language.
17 1886 Leipzig, Germany Study and intellectual development. Rizal continued observing German society and pursuing his intellectual interests while traveling through Germany.
18 1886–1887 Berlin, Germany Medicine, research, and writing. Rizal continued his medical studies and worked on his first novel, Noli Me Tangere. The novel was eventually printed in Berlin in March 1887.
19 May 13, 1887 Leitmeritz (Litoměřice), Bohemia Meeting Ferdinand Blumentritt. Rizal finally met his Austrian scholar-friend Ferdinand Blumentritt personally.
20 June 1887 Rome, Italy Historical and cultural sightseeing. Rizal visited important historical sites and monuments in Rome before returning toward Spain.
21 July 1887 Marseilles, France Transit back to the Philippines. Rizal passed through Marseilles and boarded a ship bound for the Philippines.
22 Aug. 5, 1887 Manila, Philippines First return home. After approximately five years in Europe, Rizal returned to the Philippines.
23 Aug. 1887–Feb. 1888 Calamba / Manila Medical practice and family affairs. Rizal practiced medicine and treated patients, including his mother. His novel Noli Me Tangere also caused controversy with Spanish religious authorities.
24 Feb. 3, 1888 Manila → Hong Kong Second major journey abroad. Rizal left the Philippines again because of increasing pressure and controversy surrounding Noli Me Tangere. He also intended to continue his medical and intellectual work abroad.
25 Feb. 1888 Hong Kong Medical practice, observation, and planning. Rizal observed Hong Kong society, met Filipino expatriates such as José María Basa, and later practiced medicine there.
26 Feb. 18–20, 1888 Macau Cultural observation. Rizal visited Macau with José María Basa, observing Chinese customs, celebrations, architecture, and daily life.
27 Feb. 28, 1888 Yokohama, Japan Cultural and social study. Rizal arrived in Japan and observed Japanese customs, society, language, and culture. He also stayed at the Spanish Legation.
28 March–April 1888 Tokyo, Japan Cultural study and observation. Rizal explored Japanese society, studied some of the language, and observed Japanese customs, arts, commerce, and daily life.
29 April 1888 San Francisco, California, USA American tour/observation. Rizal entered the United States and observed American society, transportation, cities, and landscapes.
30 May 11, 1888 Chicago, Illinois, USA Tour and observation. Rizal visited places of interest in Chicago during his cross-country journey. The NHCP records his arrival there on May 11, 1888.
31 May 1888 New York, New York, USA Final major stop in the United States. Rizal observed New York before leaving America for England.
32 May 24, 1888 Liverpool, England Arrival in England. Rizal arrived at Liverpool before traveling onward to London.
33 May 1888–1890 London, England Historical research and writing. This was one of the most important stops in his intellectual journey. Rizal researched Philippine history at the British Museum, studied Antonio de Morga's Sucesos de las Islas Filipinas, and prepared annotations for it.
34 1889–1890 Paris, France Research, writing, and political/intellectual work. Rizal traveled between London and Paris, writing studies and political articles and continuing his research about Philippine history and society.
35 1890 Madrid, Spain Work with Filipino reformists. Rizal frequently returned to Spain to meet Filipino intellectuals and reformists, including Marcelo H. del Pilar, Mariano Ponce, and Graciano López Jaena.
36 1891 Biarritz, France Writing. Rizal completed the manuscript of his second novel, El Filibusterismo, in France in March 1891.
37 1891 Brussels, Belgium Writing and preparation for publication. Rizal worked on and prepared El Filibusterismo for publication while experiencing financial difficulties.
38 Sept. 1891 Ghent, Belgium Publication of El Filibusterismo. With financial help from friends, Rizal had his second novel printed in Ghent.
39 1891–1892 Hong Kong Medical practice and plans for Filipino settlement. Rizal practiced medicine and developed plans connected with establishing a Filipino community, including his proposed “New Calamba” settlement in British North Borneo.
40 June 26, 1892 Manila, Philippines Final return home. Rizal returned to the Philippines from Hong Kong. He subsequently became involved in organizing La Liga Filipina.
41 July 15, 1892 Dapitan, Philippines Exile. After being arrested by Spanish authorities, Rizal was sent to Dapitan, where he would spend the next several years in exile.
